Menu names in Synthesia change from time to time. The steps below describe what to look for.

## Option 1: paste into Synthesia's AI Video Assistant ("Create a video from a prompt")
Create a 75-second, 16:9 explainer video MVP for a student web app called Bulletin. Tagline: "Find your thing." Audience: middle school, high school and college students, and school staff. Tone: friendly, upbeat and clear, like a student talking to friends. Use one friendly, casual-looking young presenter avatar with an American English voice. Visual style: clean flat design, light gray background (#F3F3F4), black text (#111113), one small red accent (#D6452F), Inter font, captions on, soft instrumental background music with no lyrics. Show the Bulletin logo at the start and the end. Use my uploaded screen recording (bulletin-demo.mp4) for the demo scene. Follow this exact structure and script:

SCENE 1, HOOK (0:00-0:06). One sentence, the avatar says: "What if finding your next club took two minutes instead of two weeks?" On-screen text: "Find your thing."

SCENE 2, PROBLEM (0:06-0:19). Avatar says: "Clubs are scattered across spreadsheets, flyers and group chats. Volunteering and programs live somewhere else. So students miss things they would love, just because they never heard about them." Visuals: a messy collage of spreadsheets, hallway flyers and chat bubbles. On-screen text: "98 clubs and teams at one school. No single place to see them."

SCENE 3, SOLUTION DEMO (0:19-0:49). Show the uploaded screen recording full screen, with the avatar small in a corner or voice only. Voiceover: "Meet Bulletin. Take a two-minute quiz and tell us what you like in your own words. Bulletin ranks every club, volunteer spot and program with a match level from one to ten. Tap Explore to see when it meets and how to join, then save your favorites." Show in order: the quiz, the ranked results with match levels, tapping Explore, saving.

SCENE 4, KEY BENEFITS (0:49-1:04). Three short text cards, about 5 seconds each, avatar says each one: "One place for school, community and online opportunities." / "Matched to you, using your own words." / "Free, simple and built by a student for students."

SCENE 5, CALL TO ACTION (1:04-1:15). Avatar says: "Ready to find your thing? Go to our website and click Take the quiz." On screen: the website address, a black "Take the quiz" button graphic, the logo and the tagline. End on the logo.

Keep every on-screen text card under 10 words.

## Option 2: build it scene by scene
Start from a blank 16:9 video or an Explainer template. Make 5 scenes. In each scene, type the avatar's line into the script box, add the on-screen text and the media listed above. Scene 3 uses the uploaded bulletin-demo.mp4 (trim it to about 30 seconds, speed it up if needed).

## Rubric check
- One-sentence hook: scene 1
- Problem (10-15 seconds): scene 2, about 13 s
- Solution demo (about 30 seconds): scene 3
- 3 short key benefits: scene 4 (convenience, USP, branding)
- Clear call to action to the landing page and Take the quiz button: scene 5
