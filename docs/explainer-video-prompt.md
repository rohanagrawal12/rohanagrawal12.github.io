## Option 1: paste into Synthesia's AI Video Assistant ("Create a video from a prompt")
Make a 75-second, 16:9 (1920x1080) animated explainer video: "Bulletin: Find your thing."
Audience: middle school, high school and college students, and school staff.
Tone: friendly, upbeat and clear, like a student talking to friends.
NO PRESENTER: No avatar, no on-screen person, no faces, no photos or stock footage of people. Voiceover only. Every scene is flat graphics, text cards and app screens.
LOOK: Flat motion graphics in the Bulletin brand, not real footage. Use only these colors: Ink #111113, Paper #F3F3F4, Charcoal #2A2A2E, Gray #6B6B72, Fog #E1E1E4, and one accent, Pin Red #D6452F. Alternate the scene backgrounds between Ink, Paper and Charcoal, like the app's welcome screens. Titles in Inter Bold, captions in Inter Regular, captions on. Slightly rounded corners (about 8px), no circles or pills. No gradients, glows, drop shadows or emoji. Keep every on-screen text card under 10 words.
BRAND MOTIF: The Bulletin logo is four rounded tiles (black, light gray, dark gray and one red) next to the lowercase word "bulletin" with a red dot on the i. Use these tiles as the recurring graphic: they slide in, and one tile turns red to mean "your match". Keep red to one small element per scene.
MEDIA: I will add three files myself in the editor, so use plain placeholders for them for now: an opening image (full screen, scene 1), my screen recording (scene 3) and my white logo (scene 5).
MOTION: Tiles slide and snap into place. Text cards fade up and hold. Gentle zoom-ins on the app screens. Soft dissolves between scenes.
STORY (use my exact narration, word for word):
0:00–0:06 HOOK, one sentence that states the promise. Ink background with a full-screen placeholder for my opening image, which already has the logo and tagline, so add no other graphics or text. Voiceover: "What if finding your next club took two minutes instead of two weeks?"
0:06–0:19 PROBLEM (13 seconds). Paper background. Gray cards drift in: a spreadsheet, a flyer and chat bubbles, piled up and messy. One small red dot sits on a card that is easy to miss. Voiceover: "Clubs are scattered across spreadsheets, flyers and group chats. Volunteering and programs live somewhere else. So students miss things they would love, just because they never heard about them." On-screen text, as two short cards: "98 clubs and teams at one school." then "No single place to see them."
0:19–0:49 SOLUTION DEMO (30 seconds). Charcoal background. Show my screen recording large in a rounded frame, with a slow zoom on the match levels. Voiceover: "Meet Bulletin. Take a two-minute quiz and tell us what you like, in your own words. Bulletin ranks every club, volunteer spot and program with a match level from one to ten, so your best matches come first. Click Explore on a card to see when it meets and how to join. Then save your favorites and find them any time in My Activities." The recording shows, in order: the quiz, ranked clubs, volunteer spots and programs with match levels, one card opened with its Explore button, and saving it.
0:49–1:04 KEY BENEFITS (3 short points, about 5 seconds each). Paper background, one card per point, each with a tile icon. Voiceover says each one, word for word: "One place for school, community and online opportunities." / "Matched to you, using your own words." / "Free, simple and built by a student for students." The three points show convenience, what makes Bulletin different, and its design and branding.
1:04–1:15 CALL TO ACTION. Ink background. Voiceover: "Ready to find your thing? Go to our website and click Take the quiz. It's free." On screen: my white logo (placeholder), the tagline "Find your thing." and a white "Take the quiz" button graphic with Ink text. Do not show a web address. End on the logo.
VOICE: A warm, friendly, confident adult male voice, American English, upbeat and clear, with natural pauses.
MUSIC & SFX: Soft instrumental background music with no lyrics, kept quiet under the voice. A soft click when the quiz and Explore are clicked, and a gentle chime when a favorite is saved.
