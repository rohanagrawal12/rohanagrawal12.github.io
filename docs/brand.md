# Bulletin brand sheet
- Personality: welcoming, organized, curious
- Value proposition: everything in one place; matched to your interests; free for students; clear details (times, rooms, deadlines)
- Mission: Helping students find clubs, volunteering and programs that fit them, so nobody misses out on something they would love just because they never heard about it.
- Colours: Ink #111113, Gray #6B6B72, Fog #E1E1E4, Paper #F3F3F4, Pin red #D6452F (optional theme: Forest #1F4D36)
- Font: Inter (logo, headings and body). Free on Google Fonts.
- Logo: four tiles in a board layout (black, two grays, and one red tile = "your match") next to the lowercase wordmark "bulletin" in Inter Bold with a red dot on the i. Files in assets/ (bulletin-logo.svg and .png, white versions, mark only, app icon).
- Tagline: Find your thing.
